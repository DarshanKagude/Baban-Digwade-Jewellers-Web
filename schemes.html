Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 11+1 Bonus Scheme work?
You pay a fixed installment every month for 11 consecutive months. On the 12th month, Baban Digwade Jewellers contributes the 12th installment amount completely FREE. You can then purchase your dream gold or silver jewellery for the total 12-month accumulated value.
What is the minimum monthly installment amount?
The minimum monthly installment starts at just ₹1,000 per month, and can be increased in multiples of ₹500 according to your preference.
How can I make monthly payments?
You can pay in person at our showroom in Pattan Kodoli or via secure UPI / Bank Transfer directly with instant WhatsApp receipt verification.
Can I redeem the scheme for gold coins or custom jewellery?
Yes! Upon completion of the scheme tenure, you can select any gold or silver jewellery from our entire showroom collection, including necklaces, bangles, rings, or custom-ordered pieces.
